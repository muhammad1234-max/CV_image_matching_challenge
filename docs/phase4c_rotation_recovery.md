# Phase 4C rotation recovery

Camera directions map first to second. Supported models are SIMPLE_PINHOLE, PINHOLE, SIMPLE_RADIAL, RADIAL and OPENCV. Distortion inversion must redistort within 0.1 px. Focal initialization is corroborated by EXIF or two disjoint, locally identifiable focal-scale estimates against the essential cubic constraint. The estimates must be within a factor 1.15 of the supplied scales and 1.10 of each other. This is conditional on the captured distortion and principal point. A stored calibrated quaternion and direction must pass triangulation and reprojection. Otherwise recover using normalized eight-point fitting and a unique viable essential decomposition. Reject collinear, planar, unsupported, ambiguous and low-parallax cases. Require eight observations, 75% positive depth, median angle ≥1 degree, and p75 reprojection error ≤2 px. No reconstructed camera pose is used. Additional calibration screens are predeclared recovery-only design choices.
