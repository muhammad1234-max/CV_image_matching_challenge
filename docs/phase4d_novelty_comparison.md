# Related work and novelty

Shi and Malik introduced normalized cuts. The inherited Laplacian relaxation and sweep are established spectral partitioning tools. [Normalized Cuts and Image Segmentation](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.868688)

Girvan and Newman used network structure to detect communities. Their edge-betweenness removal differs from the local shared-neighbor statistic here. [Community structure in social and biological networks](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.122653799)

Agreement across partitions is established in consensus clustering. B2 measures exact recurrence under monotone weight perturbations and uses it as a veto. It does not estimate co-association or provide formal stability-selection error control. [Lancichinetti and Fortunato](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep00336) [Stability selection](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2010.00740.x)

Guimera and Sales-Pardo infer edge reliability using network models. B2 uses uncalibrated boundary summaries, so its ratios cannot be interpreted as probabilities of erroneous matching. [Network reliability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2799723/)

The IMC winner already combined coarse MASt3R pre-clustering with shortlisted matching. This project tests a different intervention point after verification. The contribution demonstrated here is a reproducible abstaining boundary diagnostic on the frozen mapper input. This targeted review establishes no defensible claim of algorithmic novelty.
